# Audio credits

## Music
Claude Debussy, *Children's Corner*, L. 113 (1908). The composition is in the public domain.

- Day 1: No. 3, "Serenade for the Doll"
- Day 2: No. 5, "The Little Shepherd"
- Day 3: No. 2, "Jimbo's Lullaby"
- Day 4: No. 4, "The Snow Is Dancing"

MIDI performances (`midi/cc2.mid` to `midi/cc5.mid`): digital piano captures by Michael Bednarek,
from Wikimedia Commons, dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
`score.json` is generated from them by `tools/build_score.py`.

## Piano samples
`piano/*.mp3`: **Salamander Grand Piano V3** by **Alexander Holm**,
licensed under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)
(source: <https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3>; MP3 set from the Tone.js audio repository).

## Sound effects
All other sounds are synthesized in the browser with WebAudio.
